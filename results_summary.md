## Interpretations (derived from the computed profiles)
- **Cluster 0 (11 customers): high-value loyal.** Most recent, most frequent and highest spend.
- **Cluster 2 (40 customers): frequent regular.** Recent and frequent with moderate spend.
- **Cluster 1 (60 customers): occasional.** Reasonably recent but low order frequency and low spend.
- **Cluster 3 (30 customers): lapsed / low-engagement.** Least recent, fewest orders, lowest spend.

These labels are analyst interpretations of cluster statistics, not ground truth.

## Limitations
- The 2,000-row budget forced line items per order to be capped at 3 and orders per customer at 10,
  and excluded the very highest-frequency customers. The upper tail of Frequency and Monetary is compressed.
- Monetary covers completed, cleaned purchases only; product margin and cost are not available.
- K-Means assumes roughly spherical clusters and is sensitive to K and initialisation.
- Cluster membership can change with K, scaling and initialisation; results are indicative, not definitive.
- Clustering shows similarity, not causation.

## Possible business implications (linked to observed results)
- Retain the small high-value loyal segment (Cluster 0), which contributes disproportionate spend.
- Increase order frequency of the frequent regular and occasional segments (Clusters 2 and 1).
- Run reactivation campaigns for the lapsed segment (Cluster 3), which has the lowest recent activity.
